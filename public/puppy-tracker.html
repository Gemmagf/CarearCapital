📆A plan you’ll keep

Four or five key exercises a day, not an endless checklist. Sustainable beats ambitious with a growing puppy.

☁️Offline-first, git-synced

Data lives in the browser and syncs through a private Git repo — no backend to run, works on the trail with no signal.

01 / Why

The problem

Raising a puppy means juggling training reps, a growth curve, vet dates, meals and a hundred small “is this normal?” questions — usually across notes, spreadsheets and memory.

I wanted one calm place that answered “are we on track?” in a second, and that I could actually use one-handed on a muddy walk.

02 / What

Highlights

  • Compounding rep tracker — per-command practice history with automatic mastery levels.
  • Benchmark growth chart — weight plotted against the breed’s healthy range.
  • Sustainable daily plan — a short routine with a streak to keep it honest.
  • Care & knowledge — meals, health log, enrichment games and vetted breed facts.
03 / How

Architecture

  • React + Vite front end, installable as a PWA.
  • IndexedDB for local-first storage — instant, fully offline.
  • Git as the backend: state syncs to a private repo via the Contents API, debounced into single commits.
  • Deployed on Vercel, auto-shipping from main.
